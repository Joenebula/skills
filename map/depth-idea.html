Depth, without a 3D engine

The same 78 skills. The difference from the flat one is not a renderer — it is four cues, and every one of them is arithmetic on a z you already had to invent anyway.

Occlusion. Limbs are sorted by depth and painted far-to-near, so a near branch genuinely covers a far one. Perspective. Everything scales by f/(f+z), so near limbs are wider and further apart. Atmosphere. Distance drains opacity and pulls colour toward the ground. Focus. The far third is drawn blurred.

The tree also grows into a sphere rather than a disc — children leave their parent inside a cone in 3D, which is what stops the even fan that made the first one read as a diagram rather than a thing.
